Privacy policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

The short version. I collect only what you give me through this website, I use it to help you with AI and to stay in touch about that work, and I never sell it or send you newsletters. You can ask me to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

This website, mariablairai.com, is run by Maria Blair, AI Consulting, in Boca Raton, Florida ("I", "me"). This page explains what information I collect, why, who helps me handle it, and the choices you have.

What I collect

  • The free AI assessment: your answers, your name, your email, and your business name if you give it.
  • The free call request form: your name, your email, and, if you choose to give them, your phone number, your business name and your message.
  • Your account: if you create one to see your results or materials, your email address, which is how you sign in. There is no password.
  • Our work together: if you become a client, notes about our sessions, the recommendations I make, files I share with you, and a record of what you have paid.
  • Payments: payments go through Stripe. Stripe handles your card details; I never see or store your card number. Stripe tells me your name, your email and the amount paid.

I do not use advertising trackers or analytics tools on this website.

How I use it

  • To email you your assessment results and to reply to your call request.
  • To prepare for and run our sessions, and to share materials with you.
  • To follow up with you personally about the work you asked about.
  • To keep track of what is owed and paid.
  • To keep the forms safe from spam and automated abuse.

I do not sell your information, I do not share it with marketers, and I do not add you to a newsletter.

Who helps me run this website

A few trusted services handle information for me, only to provide their service:

  • Supabase stores the database and your account, on servers in the United States.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website and runs the "verify you are human" check on the forms.
  • Resend delivers the emails this website sends you.
  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser connects to Google when a page loads.

I may also share information if the law requires it.

Cookies and browser storage

This website remembers your light or dark display choice on your own device. If you sign in, your browser keeps you signed in until you sign out. The "verify you are human" check from Cloudflare may also use browser storage to do its job. None of this is used to track you across other websites.

How long I keep it

I keep your information while we are in touch or working together, and for as long as I need it for my business records. If you ask me to delete it, I will, unless I have to keep something, such as payment records, for tax or legal reasons.

Your choices

You can ask me to show you the information I have about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Email me at my email address and I will reply within a few business days.

Keeping it safe

Your information is stored with access limited to me. Clients who sign in can see only their own results and the files I have chosen to share with them. No system is perfect, but I take reasonable care to protect what you share.

Children

This website is meant for adults. I do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If I change this policy, I will update this page and the date at the top.
