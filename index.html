What owners ask

Does my team need to be good with technology?

No. If they can write an email, they can use AI. I teach to the least techy person in the room.

Is our information safe?

I set you up on business-grade plans and show your team exactly what the tools can see, what they keep, and what never to paste in.

Can AI answer our phones or website chat?

Yes. An AI receptionist can pick up every call, day or night, answer routine questions and book appointments. You decide what it handles and when it passes a caller to your team.

Which AI tools do you use?

Mostly Claude and ChatGPT, connected to Google Workspace or Microsoft 365. I recommend what fits your business and the tools you already pay for.
