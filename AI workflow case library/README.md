## How to add future cases

Create `002-descriptive-case-name.md`, incrementing the number for each new case. Use this structure:

1. **Executive snapshot** — title, audience, status, business problem, and core result.
2. **Before** — manual work and bottlenecks.
3. **Goal and scope** — what the workflow is designed to do.
4. **Workflow diagram** — Mermaid, with approval gates clearly represented.
5. **Agent/tool responsibilities** — human vs. automated tasks.
6. **Step-by-step operating procedure** — inputs, actions, outputs, storage.
7. **Results and evidence** — observations vs. measurements vs. estimates, with a date.
8. **Exceptions and oversight** — failures, privacy, quality checks, manual intervention.
9. **Client adaptation** — suitable industries and reusable lessons.
10. **Next steps and presentation assets** — what remains to be validated or polished.

### Evidence and presentation standards

- Clearly separate **observed** results from estimated or hypothetical time savings.
- Do not imply full automation where a human still selects, edits, publishes, or approves.
- Do not disclose client/private data, credentials, unpublished personal details, or internal links in public-facing materials.
- Prefer outcome-oriented language over tool hype.
- Cases are Markdown-first so they can later be rendered as branded HTML pages, workshop examples, PDFs, or slide decks.
- Recheck current tooling and platform permissions before implementing a similar workflow for a client.
